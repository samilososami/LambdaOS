Nimbus Sans (public/fonts/nimbus-sans-*.woff2)

Subset to WOFF2 from the URW++ base35 fonts (NimbusSans-Regular.otf, NimbusSans-Bold.otf),
the same typeface LambdaOS uses in its setup wizard, settings and lock screen.

Modification notice (AGPL-3.0 section 5): on 2026-11-25 the original
NimbusSans-Regular.otf and NimbusSans-Bold.otf were subset to Latin, Latin-1,
general punctuation and the Greek lambda, and converted to WOFF2 with
fontTools:

  python3 -m fontTools.subset NimbusSans-<Style>.otf \
    --unicodes="U+0000-00FF,U+0131,U+0152-0153,U+02C6,U+02DA,U+02DC,U+2000-206F,U+20AC,U+2122,U+2190-2199,U+2212,U+2215,U+039B,U+03BB,U+00D7,U+2022,U+2026" \
    --layout-features='kern,liga,ccmp,locl,mark,mkmk,case,tnum,lnum,frac' \
    --flavor=woff2

The WOFF2 files are served as separate font programs under the same license;
the unmodified sources are available at the link above.
