The idea.

Sami González Kamel@samilososami

It started with Watch Dogs. I was drawn to how easily Aiden Pearce and Marcus Holloway interacted with technology from a phone.

Then I discovered Kali NetHunter. I liked what it made possible, but wanted to take the idea further: a more integrated, intuitive experience, with less switching between tools.

That is how LambdaOS began, as part of Lambda Project. I’m designing a system dedicated to pentesting and cybersecurity around the Galaxy S10, from the interface down to the hardware.

LEX.

A Linux desktop on the phone.

Kali Linux in a slim XFCE session, drawn by a native viewer inside Operator, with no web layer in between. A top bar, a dock and a terminal one tap away, in about 100 MB of memory.

Development.

LambdaOS is in alpha and boots on the reference S10. There is no public download yet; the first release will ship with instructions, checksums and known issues.

  • SystemAndroid 15 on LineageOS 22.2, without Google appsWorking
  • Kernel and Wi-Fi95 modules; monitor mode verified at 2.4 GHzWorking
  • Operator and CoreCapture, WPS testing and offline cracking in developer buildsIn development
  • LEXKali desktop running on the S10In development
  • LambOn-device assistantOn hold
